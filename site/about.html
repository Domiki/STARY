About STARY

STARY brings a night of photographs together into one picture of the sky.

What STARY is

STARY is a desktop application for night-sky photographs. It cleans up your frames, lines up the stars and combines them, so that faint nebulae and galaxies come out of the noise, or so that the turning of the sky is drawn as star trails.

It follows your pictures all the way from the memory card to the finished image: calibration, alignment, stacking, masking the landscape and saving at full quality. It runs on macOS (Apple silicon) and Windows, and uses your graphics card to do the work quickly.
