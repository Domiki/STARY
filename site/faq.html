FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Read the documentation or open an issue.

What is the difference between Trails and Stacking?

Trails makes star-trail images: the frames are not aligned, and the brightest value at every pixel is kept, so each star leaves a line as the sky turns. Comet and Gap Filling are variations of it.

Stacking is for deep-sky images: the stars of every frame are aligned on top of each other and the frames are averaged, which lowers noise and reveals faint detail. Satellite trails and other outliers are rejected.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and macOS on Apple silicon. Intel Macs and Linux are not supported at the moment, although you can try building from source.

Which files can I use?

Camera RAW files (read with LibRaw, for example Fujifilm X-Trans and Sony ARW), FITS, JPEG, PNG and TIFF. Calibration with JPEG or PNG is less accurate than with RAW or FITS, and STARY shows a warning for it.

Where are my results saved?

After a run, the result is written automatically as a 32-bit float TIFF into a stary-output folder next to your first Light file, and it appears in the Result list. You can change the folder in the Operation panel. Right-click a result and choose Save as… to export TIFF, PNG, JPEG or FITS in other bit depths and color spaces.

Why is the result so dark when I open it elsewhere?

The saved result is linear: it holds the sensor values as they are, which look dark on a screen. STARY shows it stretched in its own viewer. Use Save as… with the Gamma or Auto stretch brightness option for a picture that looks right everywhere, or stretch the linear file in your editing program.

Do I need a GPU?

No. The GPU only makes stacking faster. Choose CPU or GPU in the settings; the GPU option is disabled when none is found.

Why does my system warn me when I open STARY?

Pre-release builds are not signed with a paid developer certificate. The download page explains how to open the app on macOS and Windows.

What are sessions for?

A session groups the frames that belong together: Lights plus their own Dark, Flat, Dark Flat and Bias frames. Use one session per night, camera setup or exposure. Stacking uses every session at once; the Representative image setting picks the session whose frame the others are aligned to.

What does HDR merge do?

If you keep long and short exposures of the same field in different sessions, HDR merge stacks each exposure separately and replaces the saturated parts of the long exposure with data from the short one. It is experimental and has so far been tested on synthetic data.

Does STARY send my images anywhere?

No. STARY does not upload your files or collect usage data. The only thing it asks the internet for is the list of releases on GitHub, once when it starts, to tell you when a new version is out. You can turn that off in the settings.

Is STARY free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT License. The bundled font, SUIT, is under the SIL Open Font License.
