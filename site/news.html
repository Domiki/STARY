November 2026

STARY 0.1.0 pre-release Release

The first public build of STARY for macOS (Apple silicon) and Windows.

  • Deep-sky stacking with alignment, normalization and sigma clipping
  • Star trails: Lighten, Comet and Gap Filling
  • Calibration with Dark, Flat, Dark Flat and Bias frames or masters
  • Ground masking with automatic sky detection
  • GPU acceleration, 32-bit float output and Save as… with color spaces
  • Stacking of several sessions and an experimental HDR merge
— stacking for night-sky photographs.
